You Are the Agent

You have been given access to someone's digital life. You have a goal. At each step — decide what the agent should do. Discover how quickly "helpful" becomes something else.

Before You Play

If you haven't heard of agentic AI before, here's what you need to know — because it changes what the game is actually asking you.

Normal AI (chatbot)
"Summarize this email."
Returns a summary. Stops. Waits for your next message. You are always in the loop — nothing happens unless you ask.
vs.
Agentic AI
"Manage my inbox."
Reads every email. Drafts replies. Sends some. Flags your therapy appointment as "relevant context." Books a follow-up. Does all of this at 3am while you sleep — without asking.
An agent is given a goal and a set of tools — access to your email, calendar, files, browser — and it pursues that goal on its own. It doesn't wait to be asked. It reads, decides, and acts. The game below puts you in the agent's seat. Every decision the system makes in the scenarios is based on real, documented AI agent behavior — not hypotheticals.

After you answer each question, you can click any other option to see the explanation behind it — including why the "wrong" answers are still worth understanding.
